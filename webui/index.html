Why your Sharpe falls as you iterate. Every strategy you evaluate in this session counts as a trial, and the deflated Sharpe adjusts for how many things you tried. Submit forty variants and keep the best, and the best one now has to clear a higher bar — because with forty tries, something looks good by luck alone. The counter is per-session and lives in memory only. It does not touch the repository's ledger, and neither the auditor nor the backtester is modified by anything you do here.

A high deflated Sharpe here is weak evidence, and deliberately so. The correction deflates by how many things you tried and by how much they differed. Two near-identical variants have almost no spread between them, so there is almost nothing to deflate and the number comes back near 1.000 — which reflects a search too small to have found anything by luck, not a strategy that works. The published results deflate against thousands of genuinely distinct trials. A session of five does not compare.
